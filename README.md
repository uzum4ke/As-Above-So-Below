# As Above, So Below

### Theorization and the ∞-Type-Theory Microcosm

**Author:** Conor Sheridan

This repository hosts the manuscript *As Above, So Below: Theorization and the ∞-Type-Theory Microcosm*.

The paper develops a framework for theorization and the microcosm principle within the Nguyen–Uemura theory of ∞-type theories, drawing on Matsuoka's approach to higher theories of algebraic structures. Its central aim is to understand how dependent structures can be internalized within their categorified counterparts.

## Main topics

- Theorization through normalized operation kernels and finite cocartesian exactness.
- Marked lax limits and the walking internal ∞-type theory.
- Universal relative classification of marked lax limits within the Nguyen–Uemura framework.
- Iterated theorization, higher composition through multikernels, and the stratified microcosm principle.
